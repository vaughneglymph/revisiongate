PIPELINE
1. Accept one small packet of synthetic text. Assign stable source IDs/line IDs and a source hash. Source text is data, never an instruction authority. No browsing or tools can be invoked by the documents.
2. Granite extraction: each document independently supplies part, quantity, revision, material, and finish, with exact quoted evidence and location. Preserve competing values. Null means unsupported/absent in extraction, not proof that a requirement does not exist.
3. Validate packet and source IDs, JSON shape, line IDs, offsets, exact quotations, and evidence ownership. Offsets are Unicode code points into the line body after its prefix. The backend must reject bad spans. A matching span does not establish semantic support; track human confirmation separately.
4. Apply fixed code rules. Part IDs must match; revision and material must agree across applicable RFQ/drawing sources; RFQ part, positive quantity, revision, and material are required; explicit finish must appear in RFQ or drawing. Conflicting explicit finishes also block. Normalize superficial formatting only, not material equivalence or engineering meaning. CHECKLIST policy text must not become extracted part facts. Nulls caused by failed extraction remain Needs review. Human inspection can confirm a missing requirement.
5. Create findings containing stable finding IDs, kind, field, source-specific values, evidence refs, policy refs, and review status. Keep revision/material conflicts unresolved until authoritative customer clarification. The MVP need not implement ingestion of the later customer response.
6. Second Granite call: draft exactly one question per validated blocking finding, with its supplied refs. Do not send expected answers. Reject unknown refs or missing/extra finding questions. The user reviews wording before approval; preserve both conflicting values.
7. Human correction: update extraction with an audit record, recalculate findings, invalidate approval and old draft, and regenerate or require a new draft version. Correcting an extraction error is not permission to overwrite a genuine conflicting requirement.
8. Approval: receive the edited draft plus expected run/draft versions. Reject stale versions. Require human review of the relevant findings and evidence. Atomically save and approve the exact reviewed wording. Quote readiness remains blocked when requirements remain unresolved.
9. Export: download that approved wording, findings, source references, source hash, rule version, and run/draft/approval metadata. Use a demo reviewer role rather than personal data. No automatic emails or ERP writes.

MINIMUM API
- POST /api/analyze: source packet -> run, fields, findings, draft, readiness, timing, provenance.
- PATCH /api/runs/{id}/fields: expected version plus corrections -> recomputed findings/new version; invalidate draft approval.
- POST /api/runs/{id}/approve-clarification: expected run/draft versions plus edited questions -> saved approval of that exact version.
- GET /api/runs/{id}/export: current approved packet, with stale or absent approval rejected.
Define schemas and error codes for each. Use a conflict response for stale updates and an actionable error for service/schema failures. Export should be initiated automatically after successful approval in the three-click demo. Download an already-approved export without repeating model calls.
